Sign in to arena.ai

The sign-in window is part of the desktop app (arena-ai.exe). Here, use the steps below.

Opens arena.ai inside the app. Sign in with Google or email; the app picks up the session by itself.

or copy the session from your browser
  1. Open arena.ai in Chrome, Edge or Brave and sign in.
  2. Press F12 and open the Console tab.
  3. Type copy(document.cookie) and press Enter.
    If the console blocks pasting, type allow pasting first.
  4. Come back here and press Paste from clipboard.
More options

The session stays on this computer (in ~/.arena-cli). Signing out of arena.ai ends it.
